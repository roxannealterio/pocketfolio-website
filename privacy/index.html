Privacy Policy

Pocketfolio (ABN 80 241 124 181) (we, us or our) is bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy explains how we manage your personal information. Consumer Data Right data is also covered by our CDR Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect your name, email address, date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or over) and login details; your answer to "how did you hear about us" if you give one; information you enter about your accounts, assets, liabilities and goals; information you allow us to receive from crypto exchanges, wallet addresses, online stores and payment platforms you connect, such as holdings, sales and payouts; financial data you authorise us to receive from your banks and other providers, such as balances and transactions; and technical information such as device type and app usage.

2. How we collect it

We collect information directly from you, and from third parties you authorise, including financial institutions through the Consumer Data Right. We do not ask for or store your bank passwords.

Your profile photo stays on your device and is not uploaded to us.

3. Why we use it

We use your information to provide and secure the Service, show your financial position, categorise transactions, detect recurring payments, send notifications you have chosen, respond to you, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Automated processing

We use computer programs that process your transaction and account data to categorise spending, match transfers and detect recurring payments. These programs do not make decisions that significantly affect your rights or interests.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Service, and where required or authorised by law. They are:

  • Basiq: our Consumer Data Right data provider, for bank, card, loan and super connections.
  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and hosting provider. Your account and financial data are stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, and subscription payments. We never receive your card details.
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of which subscription you have. It receives an anonymous account ID and your purchase history, not your financial data.
  • CoinGecko and market data providers: supply crypto and share prices. They receive only the coin or ticker names, never who you are.
  • The exchanges, wallets and stores you connect: these include Swyftx, CoinSpot, Independent Reserve, Binance, Shopify, Stripe, Square and PayPal. We read your balances and sales from them using read-only access you set up.

Overseas disclosure. Your account and financial data are stored in Australia. Some providers process limited information outside Australia: RevenueCat (United States) receives an anonymous account ID and purchase history; Apple and Google (United States) handle sign-in and subscription payments; CoinGecko and market data providers (outside Australia) receive only coin or ticker names; and exchanges, wallets and stores you connect, such as Binance, Stripe, PayPal and Shopify, may be located outside Australia and only send us data you have authorised.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites. If you share a goal, the people you invite see the goal, its balance and its contributions.

6. Marketing

We will only send you marketing with your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time.

7. Security and retention

We protect information with encryption in transit and at rest, and with access controls that let each person see only their own data. Keys you enter for exchanges and stores are encrypted on our server before they are stored, are never sent back to the app, and can only read information, not move money. Your profile photo stays on your phone.

We keep information only for as long as we need it for these purposes or as the law requires, and then delete or de-identify it.

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile › Settings › Delete everything, or by logging in at pocketfolio.com.au/account. This permanently deletes your account, your saved entries and connections, and your bank data held by our CDR data provider, and it revokes Sign in with Apple. It does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel that in your device's subscription settings.

8. Access and correction

You may ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. You can also export or delete your data in Settings.

9. Complaints

If you have a privacy complaint, contact us at support@pocketfolio.com.au. We will acknowledge it within 2 business days and aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will publish the current version here. Contact our privacy officer at support@pocketfolio.com.au or PO Box 647, Mareeba QLD 4880.
