Questions

Still wondering about something? Email support@pocketfolio.com.au and a real person will reply.

Is it safe to connect my bank?

You sign in on your bank's own page and approve what's shared. Pocketfolio never sees or stores your password, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Can I link more than one bank?

Yes. Connect every bank, card, loan and super account you hold, in any combination. Each bank is a separate consent you control, and you choose which accounts at that bank to share.

I run an online store. Can I see that too?

Yes. Connect Shopify, Stripe, Square or PayPal with a read-only key, and your sales, payouts and fees sit next to your personal accounts. You can also add a business as an asset and update its value yourself.

Can I see my super too?

Yes. Add each super fund with its balance and Pocketfolio includes it in your net worth, with a reminder to update it when your statement arrives. Super funds aren't part of the Consumer Data Right yet, so they can't be connected automatically like your bank.

Which banks can I connect?

Pocketfolio connects through Basiq, an accredited Consumer Data Right provider. Australia's major banks are supported, and anything else can be added manually.

Is Pocketfolio financial advice?

No. Pocketfolio shows you information about your own money. It doesn't recommend financial products. Speak to a licensed adviser for personal advice.

Can Pocketfolio move my money?

No. Pocketfolio reads data you choose to share. It can't make payments or transfers.

What happens if I disconnect a bank?

Sharing stops straight away and the data we received for that account is deleted.

Do you sell my data?

No. Pocketfolio is free to download, and Pocketfolio Plus unlocks every connection and feature, with a 14-day free trial. We never sell your data.

How much does it cost?

Pocketfolio is free to download. Pocketfolio Plus is A$79 a year with a 14-day free trial, or A$9.99 a month. Prices include GST, and you can cancel any time in your App Store settings.

Do I still need an accountant?

For tax and advice, yes. Pocketfolio keeps your records organised and easy to export, so your accountant gets what they need faster.
