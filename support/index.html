A bank says it needs reconnecting

Access to your bank through the Consumer Data Right has to be renewed from time to time. Open the app, tap the bank's Reconnect card and follow your bank's steps. Your balances update within a minute.

Connecting an exchange, wallet or store

Go to Portfolio › Crypto or Business and tap Add. Use a read-only key, and the app shows the exact steps for each provider. Never enter a seed phrase, a private key or a Stripe secret key.

Subscriptions, trials and refunds

Pocketfolio Plus is billed by Apple or Google. To cancel, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone, or Play Store › Payments & subscriptions on Android. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google. If you reinstall, use Restore purchases in Profile › Settings.

Sharing a goal with your partner

Open the goal and tap Invite. Your partner taps Goals › Join with a code and enters the code you send them.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, go to Profile › Settings › Delete everything, or log in at pocketfolio.com.au/account. This permanently deletes your account and all your data, including bank data held by our data provider. Cancel any subscription first. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
