CDR Policy

Pocketfolio uses the Consumer Data Right (CDR) to receive data from your banks with your consent.

1. Who collects your bank data

Bank connections in Pocketfolio are provided by Basiq Pty Ltd, an accredited data recipient under the CDR. When you connect a bank, Basiq asks for your consent on its own secure consent screen, collects the data you agree to share from your bank, and provides it to Pocketfolio to show in the app. You log in at your bank, and Pocketfolio never sees or stores your bank password.

Basiq's handling of CDR data is described in its CDR Policy at docs.basiq.io, and its accreditation can be checked on the CDR Register at cdr.gov.au. The consent screen shows these details before you share any data.

2. How CDR data is handled

What we receive. Only the data you consent to share, such as account names, balances and transactions, for the accounts you select.

Why. To show your accounts, balances, spending and net worth in Pocketfolio. We do not use CDR data for any other purpose without your consent, and we never sell it.

Multiple banks. You may connect up to 2 institutions on the free plan, or as many as you like with Pocketfolio Plus. Each has its own consent. You can withdraw it at any time through your bank's data sharing dashboard, by contacting us, or by deleting your Pocketfolio account.

Withdrawing consent. If you withdraw consent or it expires, we stop collecting data from that institution and delete the CDR data we received under that consent, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Where it's stored. CDR data is stored in Australia and is not disclosed overseas.

3. Access, correction and complaints

To request access to or correction of your CDR data, or to make a complaint, contact us at support@pocketfolio.com.au. We will acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Australian Financial Complaints Authority at afca.org.au or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
